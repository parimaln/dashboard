Privacy Policy for Kakarii
Effective Date: 23 Aug 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Kakarii ("we," "us," or "our") handles your information when you use the Kakarii mobile application (the "App").
1. Our Privacy Philosophy
Kakarii was built with privacy as a core principle. The App is designed to be fully offline-first. This means your personal journal entries, tasks, goals, and moods are yours and yours alone.
2. Information Collection and Storage
We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers.
•
Local Storage: All entries (Tasks, Events, Notes, Ideas), #tags, and mood logs are stored locally on your device in a private database.
•
No Accounts: Kakarii does not require you to create an account or provide an email address, name, or phone number.
•
No Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs to monitor your behavior within the App.
3. Device Permissions
To provide its core features, Kakarii may request the following permissions:
•
Speech Recognition & Microphone: Used solely to convert your voice to text for "Voice Capture" entries. The audio is processed by your device's system speech recognizer and is never recorded or stored by Kakarii.
•
Notifications: Used to provide "Daily Catch-up" reminders at a time you specify.
•
Receive Shared Data: Used when you choose to share text from another application into Kakarii to create an entry.
4. Third-Party Services
Because Kakarii is an offline app, it does not communicate with any third-party services, except for:
•
Android System Services: For features like speech-to-text or system notifications.
•
Google Play Services: For basic app delivery and updates via the Google Play Store.
5. Data Retention and Deletion
Since all data is stored on your device, you have complete control over it:
•
Manual Deletion: You can delete individual entries or tags within the App at any time.
•
App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the Kakarii App will typically delete all data stored within the App's local database.
•
Backups: If you use Android's system-level backup (Google Drive), your encrypted App data may be backed up by the Android system according to your device settings.
6. Children's Privacy
Kakarii does not knowingly collect any personal information. Because no data is transmitted from the device, the App is inherently safe for users of all ages.
7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If we introduce cloud sync or account features in the future, we will notify you within the App and require your explicit consent before any data leaves your device.

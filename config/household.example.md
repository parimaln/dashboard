# Household notes

Standing context for the daily briefing. Edit this once and the briefing takes it
into account every day; it is the place for the recurring things that never appear
in a calendar or a chore list.

The model is given this file verbatim along with today's date and weekday, and is
told to mention an item **only on the days it actually applies**. Write it however
you like — the headings below are a suggestion, not a schema.

## People

- **Ada** (7) — walks to school. Gets cold quickly; check she has mittens.
- **Sam** (11) — cycles unless it is icy.
- **Ellen** — works from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday** — Ada has chess club after school. She needs her chess book and her
  club card.
- **Tuesday** — bins go out the night before. Sam has football; boots and shin pads.
- **Wednesday** — Ada swims. Swimming bag, towel, goggles, and a snack for after.
- **Thursday** — Ellen is at the office, so school pickup is Parimal's.
- **Friday** — library books are due back.
- **Sunday** — laundry day; check everyone's kit is washed for Monday.

## Standing reminders

- If it is below freezing, Sam takes the bus rather than cycling.
- Parcel deliveries go to the neighbour at number 14.
- If dinner needs something we do not have, say so before the school run, not after.

## Preferences

- Keep the briefing short. We read it while putting shoes on.
- Norwegian public holidays: assume school is closed.
